MANARA GROUP WEBSITE
=====================

Files:
- index.html
- assets/manara-logo.png

Upload:
1. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
2. Upload index.html and the assets folder into your server's public_html folder.
3. Open your domain.

Notes:
- The page is responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Seafood photography is loaded from Unsplash URLs, so the server needs internet access for those images.
- Replace the placeholder contact details, statistics and product information with Manara Group's verified details before publishing.
